Each followed wallet is polled directly (rotating batch per cycle) and its swaps also surface in the main DEX stream. When a followed wallet buys, the copywallet strategy opens a paper position; when it sells, the position mirrors the exit (plus the strategy's stop/trail as a safety net). 🎯 Alpha Signals confluence score · high-conviction
The Alpha Engine scores every live token 0–100 by confluence: proven-wallet buying (each wallet auto-graded by the realized forward-return of what it buys), buy acceleration, net-flow, liquidity growth, organic-buyer ratio (anti-wash), and momentum. It only auto-buys above the bar (default 68), sizes bigger on higher conviction, then scales out partial profit and lets a runner ride to capture the fat tail. Tune the score bar and scale-out in Trading. Paper by default; 🔴 LIVE routes on-chain. Not advice.

Every swap and trade is preserved in IndexedDB (up to a 10 GB target; the browser grants quota from free disk, shown above). On reload, recent history is restored so Trends and strategies keep context, and the full dataset stays on disk. Export downloads everything as NDJSON for offline analysis / strategy backtesting. localStorage can't hold this (≈5 MB cap) — IndexedDB can. For best durability, serve via python3 -m http.server and grant storage when prompted.
