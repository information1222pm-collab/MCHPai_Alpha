# 08 — Weapons, Poisons & Augs (the DPS you're probably leaving on the table)

Macros and positioning are most of your DPS — but a returning Rogue almost
always has easy, permanent gains sitting in these three areas. Fix them once and
every parse goes up forever.

## Weapons — non-negotiables

- **Dual-wield two ONE-HAND PIERCING weapons.** Rogue damage AAs only boost
  *piercing* damage, and **Backstab requires a piercing weapon in your primary
  hand.** A slashing/blunt main hand quietly throws away a huge chunk of your
  damage. This is the #1 silent gear mistake for returning Rogues.
- **Ratio matters.** For your *main hand*, favor a strong **damage/delay ratio**
  (high damage, reasonable delay) since Backstab and your big hits scale off main
  hand damage. Off-hand just wants good ratio for proc/poison frequency.
- **Slot damage augments** in both weapons. Pure-DPS augs (high raw damage /
  damage-focus) are usually your best slots. Don't leave aug slots empty.

## Poisons — a real, free DPS line most returners ignore

Modern Rogue poisons are genuine sustained DPS, not a gimmick. Key rules:

- **You can run TWO poisons at once: one DPS poison + one utility poison.**
- **Poison procs on your piercing attacks (your normal swings) — NOT on
  Backstab.** It's applied to your **primary weapon** slot.
- Keep them **always applied**; they fall off and need re-applying. Make a
  one-click "apply poison" hotkey.

**DPS poisons (pick one to keep up):**
| Poison | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Nigriventor** | Direct-damage proc (solid all-purpose DPS) |
| **Choresine** | Massive single-use direct damage (burst) |
| **Taipan** | DD that procs more often for less per hit |
| **Mamba** | Self-only DD |

**Utility poisons (run one alongside your DPS poison):**
Muscimol (aggro reduction — great for not pulling off the tank during a burn),
Oleander (slow), Larkspur (snare), plus various debuffs.

> Can't make them yourself? They're tradeable — many Rogues just buy poisons in
> bulk from tradeskillers. A stack lasts a long time. The DPS-per-effort here is
> excellent.

## Aggro management (so your burn doesn't get you killed)

You flagged "less stress." A Rogue who out-aggros the tank mid-burn dies and
parses zero. Two cheap fixes:

- **Sleight of Hand / Escape line** — Rogues have a strong hate-shed AA. Keep it
  on your bar for "oops" moments.
- Run a **Muscimol** utility poison during heavy burns to passively bleed aggro.
- **Open a beat or two AFTER the tank engages**, not before. Let threat build.

## Haste & buffs (have the Shaman cover these)

- Stay **at/near the haste cap** — your Shaman's haste/Talisman line plus gear
  should get you there. More swings = more poison procs and auto damage.
- Make sure you're getting the Shaman's **Malo** (lowers mob resists → your
  poisons/debuffs land harder) and any group melee-damage buffs.

## Throwing (your "can't get behind it" plan)

- Keep a stack of **throwing weapons** in your ranged slot and a throwing/ranged
  burn disc handy. On frontal-cleave or wall-hugging mobs you switch to ranged
  instead of eating hits from the front. Less than backstab, far more than zero.

## The 5-minute permanent-gains checklist

- [ ] Two 1H **piercing** weapons equipped (piercing in main hand).
- [ ] **Damage augs** in both weapon aug slots.
- [ ] A **DPS poison** + a **utility poison** applied, with a re-apply hotkey.
- [ ] **Haste capped** (Shaman haste up).
- [ ] A **hate-shed** AA on your bar.
- [ ] Throwing stack + ranged disc ready for frontal fights.

Tick all six and you've locked in DPS that shows up on *every* fight, before you
even press a burn key.
