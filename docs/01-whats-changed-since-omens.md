# 01 — What Changed Since Omens of War

You left around Omens (2004, level cap 70). It's now **Shattering of Ro (SoR)**,
the 32nd expansion (Dec 2025, **level cap 130**). Here's the honest catch-up,
Rogue-focused, no fluff.

## The big-picture changes

- **Level cap 70 → 130, and AAs exploded.** "Full AAs" now means thousands of
  points. The good news: as a returning player you already have them, so you
  don't grind — you just need to **slot and use the right activated AAs**.
- **Disciplines are now your DPS engine.** In Omens, discs were occasional
  (Aggressive, Combat Fury, etc.). Now Rogues have **stackable layers**: one
  big *offensive discipline* running at a time, **plus** a stack of activated
  **AA abilities** that all fire during a burn. Knowing which is a "disc" (only
  one at a time) vs. an "AA" (fires independently) is the whole game. See
  [`04-burn-and-cooldown-reference.md`](04-burn-and-cooldown-reference.md).
- **Backstab is automated-ish.** You still must be **behind the mob**, but
  Backstab fires far more often and **Assassinate** procs off it (huge spike
  damage on non-immune targets). Positioning matters more than ever.
- **Ranged matters now.** Throwing has real burn discs (the "Fatal Aim" /
  ranged line). On fights where you can't get behind the mob, you throw.
- **Spells/discs come in Ranks (Rk. I / II / III).** Rk. I from vendors,
  II/III from drops and raids. Always slot the highest rank you own.
- **Heroic stats, modern gear, augments.** Your weapons want **damage augments**
  and you want your **epic-equivalent / clicky** burn items on a hotkey.
  Piercing weapons are still mandatory for Backstab.

## What this means for *you* specifically

You're a 130 Rogue with full AAs boxing a Shaman, aiming at SoR group + raid.
Practically:

1. **You don't need to grind anything to be effective.** You need
   **organization**: the right AAs slotted, a fixed burn order, and cooldown
   visibility. That's a setup problem, not a gear/time problem — which is good,
   because setup is exactly what this package fixes.
2. **Your DPS "rotation" is mostly passive.** Auto-attack + Backstab is the
   bulk. Burns are spikes you layer on top. So "DPS inconsistency" almost always
   comes from one of: not behind the mob, attack toggled off on a target swap,
   or sitting on ready cooldowns. All three are addressed here.
3. **The Shaman is a force multiplier.** Slow keeps you alive; **Malo** and the
   Shaman's debuffs raise your damage; **haste/Talisman** buffs and **Cannibalize**
   keep the Shaman ticking. You'll drive all of it from the Rogue.

## Vocabulary refresher (things you'll see in this package)

| Term | What it means now |
|------|-------------------|
| **Disc** | Discipline. **Only one offensive disc runs at a time.** Fire-and-replace. |
| **AA (activated)** | An Alternate Advancement ability with its own cooldown. **Stacks with discs and other AAs** — fire them all during a burn. |
| **Combat Ability** | Disc-like abilities on the Combat Abilities window; cast with `/disc` in MQ2. |
| **Burn** | The window where you fire every cooldown at once for a damage spike. |
| **Spire** | A long-cooldown AA (First/Second/Third Spire of the Rake) that boosts crits. Third Spire is the damage one. |
| **Synergy / Twisted Chance** | A Rogue mechanic where a melee proc opens a damage-amp window — "Bestow/Twisted Chance" AAs. |
| **Rk. II / III** | Higher ranks of the same spell/disc. Slot the best you own. |
