# Future Research

`research/` is the chaotic counterpart to stable `services/`. Ideas incubate here
and in `notebooks/`; only proven ones graduate into `libraries/` + `services/`.

The exotic domains in `libraries/` are deliberate long-term bets — the platform
treats them as first-class so they can deepen for years.

## Entropy theory (`research/entropy_theory`)

Beyond buyer entropy: transfer-entropy between wallets (who leads whom),
mutual-information feature selection, and minimum-description-length detection of
coordinated behavior.

## Epidemiology (`research/epidemiology`)

Full SEIR with a contact network = the actual wallet graph; time-varying R0;
super-spreader (influencer wallet) identification; forecasting the infection
curve to time entries/exits.

## Game theory (`research/game_theory`)

The tip auction as an all-pay auction; sniper-vs-sniper equilibria; optimal
bluffing/feinting; mechanism-aware execution that anticipates competitors.

## Ecology (`research/ecology`)

Lotka-Volterra predator/prey calibrated to real predator (smart money) and prey
(retail) populations; niche partitioning of wallet archetypes; ecosystem-health
indices as macro regime signals.

## Astronomy (`research/astronomy`)

N-body "capital gravity" simulation; orbital-mechanics models of price
trajectories; detecting "dark matter" (hidden coordinated capital) by its
gravitational effect on observable flows.

## Reinforcement learning (`research/reinforcement_learning`)

Policy learning for execution and portfolio allocation in a high-fidelity market
simulator before any shadow/canary on-chain.

## Graph neural networks (`research/graph_neural_networks`)

Temporal GNNs over the evolving wallet graph; link prediction (who will co-buy
next); inductive embeddings for never-before-seen wallets.

## Multi-agent systems (`research/multi_agent`)

Agentic simulation of creators, snipers, bots, MMs, and retail to backtest
strategies against a *reactive* market and to stress-test risk controls.

## Promotion criteria

An idea graduates when it (1) has a clean interface, (2) beats the incumbent
out-of-sample after costs, (3) has tests + observability, and (4) doesn't
destabilize the production contract (schemas/events).
