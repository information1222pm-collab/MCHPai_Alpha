=======================================================================
 SHAMAN IN-GAME SOCIAL HOTKEYS  -  Cloudi's box  (no MQ2 required)
=======================================================================
Keep the Shaman window in a corner of your ultrawide. These let you tab
over and handle the few Shaman things that matter for the Rogue's DPS &
survival with one click each. Same setup steps as the Rogue file.

Replace spell names with the HIGHEST RANK you own (see your spellbook).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 1) SLOW  -  keeps you and the tank alive = longer fights = more DPS
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  SLOW
LINE1: /assist ROGUENAME
LINE2: /pause 2
LINE3: /cast "Turgur's Swarm"        | <- your highest slow

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 2) MALO  -  lowers mob resists; helps your debuffs/poisons + raid land
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  MALO
LINE1: /assist ROGUENAME
LINE2: /pause 2
LINE3: /cast "Malosenia"             | <- your highest malo

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 3) HEAL  -  quick heal on the Rogue (or tank in groups)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  HEAL
LINE1: /target ROGUENAME
LINE2: /pause 2
LINE3: /cast "<your fast/group heal>"

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 4) CANNI  -  HP -> mana so you never go OOM mid-fight
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  CANNI
LINE1: /cast "Cannibalization"       | <- your highest canni / use the AA

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 5) HASTE/BUFF  -  buff the Rogue (run once after zoning/death)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  BUFF ROGUE
LINE1: /target ROGUENAME
LINE2: /pause 2
LINE3: /cast "<Talisman / haste line>"
LINE4: /pause 45
LINE5: /cast "<2nd buff>"
   (chain more buffs across additional BUFF2/BUFF3 socials as needed.)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 6) REZ  -  rez your Rogue's corpse after a wipe (corpse recovery)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  REZ
LINE1: /corpse                       | (or target the corpse manually)
LINE2: /cast "<your rez spell / Incarnate Anew>"
   (Target the Rogue's corpse first, then press. On most setups: Rogue
    runs back / summons corpse, targets it, Shaman casts rez.)

=======================================================================
 SHAMAN QUICK FLOW DURING A FIGHT
=======================================================================
  Pull lands -> SLOW -> MALO -> then just HEAL as needed and CANNI when
  mana dips. Everything else (DoTs, roar) is bonus damage when you have
  spare time. For fully hands-off, run a Shaman heal/slow bot routine and
  let the Rogue's BoxAssist.mac just keep the Shaman on-target.
=======================================================================
