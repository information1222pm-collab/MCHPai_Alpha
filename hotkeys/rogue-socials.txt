=======================================================================
 ROGUE IN-GAME SOCIAL HOTKEYS  -  Cloudi  (no MQ2 required)
=======================================================================
These work on ANY server, including live. To use one:
  1. Open the Socials window (EQ button -> Socials, or default 'O').
  2. Right-click an empty social button -> edit.
  3. Set the NAME and paste the LINES (max 5 lines per social).
  4. Drag the finished social onto a hotbar button.

Color tip for a clean bar: name them in CAPS so they read fast at a glance.

NOTES ON ABILITY LINES
  * Discs:  /disc <Exact Disc Name>          (names: see docs/04)
  * AAs:    drag the AA from your AA window straight onto a hotbar button -
            it's one click and needs no social. For a MULTI-AA burn social,
            EQ requires /alt activate <numeric ID>; fill IDs in the BURN
            template below, OR just place the 4-5 burn AAs on one hotbar row
            and press them L-to-R. Both are fine; the row is less setup.
  * Backstab: /doability "Backstab"  (if that errors on your client, drag
            Backstab onto an Abilities button and use /doability <slot#>).

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 1) ASSIST  -  swap to the tank's target AND start attacking
    (fixes "attack toggles off on swaps" + covers Swapping Targets)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  ASSIST
LINE1: /assist TANKNAME
LINE2: /attack on
LINE3: /pause 2
LINE4: /doability "Backstab"
   (replace TANKNAME with your main assist / tank. Boxed solo: use your
    Shaman's name or remove line 1 and just /assist on your current target.)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 2) SPAM  -  your sustained "do damage" key. Mash it during fights.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  SPAM
LINE1: /attack on
LINE2: /doability "Backstab"
LINE3: /disc Frenzied Stabbing Discipline
   (line 3 keeps an offensive disc up; it just no-ops if one is already
    running or on cooldown. Swap to your highest-rank disc name.)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 3) BURN  -  fire your big cooldowns. (Fill in your AA IDs once.)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  BURN
LINE1: /alt activate 0000    | Third Spire of the Rake
LINE2: /alt activate 0000    | Rogue's Fury
LINE3: /alt activate 0000    | Twisted Chance
LINE4: /disc Frenzied Stabbing Discipline
LINE5: /doability "Backstab"
   HOW TO GET THE IDs: hover the AA in your AA window, or it's faster to
   just drag Spire / Rogue's Fury / Twisted Chance / Ligament Slice onto a
   hotbar row and press them in order. (See docs/04 for finding IDs.)
   NOTE: a social is max 5 lines - put the rest of your AAs (Ligament Slice,
   Twisted Shank, etc.) on a second BURN2 social or on the hotbar row.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 4) DEBUFF  -  open every fight on a named with this
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  DEBUFF
LINE1: /alt activate 0000    | Ligament Slice  (fill in ID, or drag to bar)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 5) RANGED  -  when you CAN'T get behind the mob, throw instead
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  RANGED
LINE1: /autofire on
LINE2: /disc <your throwing / Fatal Aim disc name>
   (make sure throwing weapons/stacks are in your ranged slot)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 6) CORPSE  -  corpse recovery (you flagged this as a top action)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  CORPSE
LINE1: /corpse
LINE2: /say Pulling my corpse - need a rez when ready
   (Stand on/near your corpse and press it to drag it to you. For rez,
    target your corpse and have the Shaman cast - see REZHELP below.)

NAME:  CONSENT
LINE1: /consent SHAMANNAME
   (lets your Shaman drag/loot your corpse. Replace SHAMANNAME.)

NAME:  FINDCORPSE
LINE1: /say (use the in-game /findcorpse or the Find feature; on most
        servers corpses summon to you with the guild/expansion corpse
        summoner - keep one handy.)

-----------------------------------------------------------------------
 7) PULL  -  simple ranged pull (if you end up pulling)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
NAME:  PULL
LINE1: /target nearest
LINE2: /autofire on
LINE3: /say Pulling %t

=======================================================================
 RECOMMENDED HOTBAR LAYOUT (small-medium, ultrawide friendly)
=======================================================================
 Row 1 (left thumb keys):   ASSIST | SPAM | BURN | DEBUFF | RANGED
 Row 2 (burn AAs, drag-on): Spire | Rogue's Fury | Twisted Chance |
                            Ligament Slice | Twisted Shank
 Row 3 (utility):           CORPSE | CONSENT | Evade | Sneak | Hide
 Keep it to ~3 rows. That alone kills the "too many buttons" problem.
=======================================================================
