Thanks for your order! This is a complete, custom 8-box build for your
EverQuest teams, built entirely around ISBoxer (no MacroQuest). Everything
is designed so ONE person can drive a full group: one key assists, one key
burns, one key panics, one key pulls.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE 6 KEYS YOU ACTUALLY PRESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  ~  (tilde) ... ASSIST + ATTACK  - every DPS box targets Tonga's target,
                 starts attacking, and sends pets in. Kills slow swapping.
  F1 ......... BURN (stepped)     - press 1 = epics/long cooldowns,
                 press 2 = class burn discs/AA, press 3 = nuke rotation.
                 One button walks the whole group's burn.
  F2 ......... PANIC              - both healers dump their biggest
                 emergency heal and the tank pops a defensive. Bind it to
                 a mouse button for reflex use.
  G  ......... PULL               - only your puller acts (Miklo on Live,
                 Strangeanevil on TLP). The group holds and doesn't aggro.
  F5 ......... BUFF               - re-fires group buffs, songs and clickies.
  \  ......... FOLLOW             - the whole team sticks to Tonga to reform.
  Pause ...... BROADCAST ON/OFF   - safety key: turn key-broadcasting off
                 before you move a single box so it doesn't echo to all 8.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE AWARENESS LAYER (buffs / procs / named / audio / overlays)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Buff tracking ..... EQ Audio Triggers fire on "has worn off"; the Command
                      Center shows live countdown bars that go amber then
                      alert when a buff drops - your cue to press F5.
  Proc notifications  Audio Triggers on key procs play a distinct chime;
                      the proc monitor counts them for the fight.
  Named spawn alerts  Armed spawn names trigger a loud tone and ISBoxer
                      pops the matching box to the front so you see it.
  Audio warnings .... Distinct tones for named / proc / buff / panic, with
                      a master on/off and volume.
  Class overlays .... Each box shows what its role needs: tank defensives &
                      aggro hold, healer mana & panic state, DPS burn
                      readiness & pet health.
  All of it is native ISBoxer + EQ Audio Triggers. No MacroQuest, nothing
  injected into the game.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO SET UP (about 30-40 minutes, once)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click Launch-Navigator.bat, then click INSTALL.
  2. Open the Command Center (button in the navigator) - your reference.
  3. Build ISBoxer from ISBoxer-Profiles\ (recipe, ~10 min), then
     "Export to Inner Space" and launch a team.
  4. In EverQuest, paste the socials from EQ-Macros\<team>\ - one file per
     box. Drag each social to the hotbar slot shown (~, F1, F2, G).
  5. Add the audio triggers from EQ-AudioTriggers\ (in-game, Alt+A).
  6. Standardize your bags using Inventory\.
  7. Test on easy mobs: ~ assist, F1 burn, F2 panic, G pull. Done!


--------------------------------------------------------------------
BACKUPS & RESTORE - TRY IT RISK-FREE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  Before changing anything, the installer backs up:
    * your ISBoxer Toolkit.xml, and
    * your EverQuest UI (window layouts + uifiles\ skins).
  Don't like the new setup? Open the navigator > Backups & Restore >
  "Restore my old EQ UI" (close EverQuest first). Or open the newest
  Documents\MCHP-Boxing\Backups\EQ-UI-* folder and run Restore-EQ-UI.bat.
  Nothing is destructive - the build mostly ADDS socials and hotbuttons.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
ONE HONEST NOTE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  EverQuest stores in-game socials and hotbuttons on Daybreak's servers,
  per character - so no tool (this one or any other) can silently write
  them into the game. That's why your socials are pre-built as files: it
  turns an hour of typing into a quick copy-paste. Everything else is
  automated for you.
