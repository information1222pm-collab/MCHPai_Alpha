🗡️ Rogue DPS Cheat Card

Shattering of Ro · Lv 130 keep next to the keyboard

The whole job (memorize this)

  1. Be BEHIND the mob — ~half your damage.
  2. Keep Auto-Attack + Backstab going.
  3. Fire burns on cooldown.
  4. Don't stand in bad stuff.
If a parse is low, it's almost always #1 (positioning) or attack toggled off on a target swap.

Your keys

  • Alt+2 ASSIST / SPAM loop — press once at pull, leave it.
  • Alt+1 BURN — at pull & every time it's back up.
  • Alt+3 Shaman driver (slow + malo).
  • Alt+0 Stop macros.
No-MQ2: mash SPAM, hit BURN on named. Same idea.

Burn order (BURN key does this)

  1. Rogue's Fury
  2. Third Spire of the Rake
  3. Lead disc — Frenzied Stabbing or Twisted Chance
  4. Ligament Slice
  5. Rake's Rampage + ready damage AAs
  6. Clicky burn item(s)
Disc fades mid-fight → next disc: Executioner's → Knifeplay → Razor's Edge → Arcworker. (RogueAssist auto-cycles this.)

Sustained loop (always on)

  • Auto-attack ON the kill target.
  • Backstab on cooldown.
  • One offensive disc ALWAYS running.
  • Ligament Slice debuff up.
  • Can't get behind it? Throw instead.

Permanent gains (set once)

  • 2× one-hand PIERCING (pierce in main hand).
  • Damage augs in both weapons.
  • DPS poison + utility poison applied (procs on swings, not BS).
  • Haste capped (Shaman haste up).
  • Hate-shed AA on bar for burns.

Shaman (box support)

  • SLOW + MALO on every fight.
  • HEAL you/tank; CANNI when low.
  • REZ hotkey for corpse recovery.
  • Keep haste/buffs on the Rogue.

Fix-it checklist when DPS feels off

  • Behind the mob the whole time?
  • Attack stayed ON across swaps?
  • Sat on a ready burn? (watch the HUD)
  • Disc always running (no empty slot)?
  • Mob slowed + malo'd + Ligament Slice?
  • Piercing main hand + augs?
  • Both poisons applied?
  • Opened after the tank had threat?
