# research/

The chaotic R&D layer — intentionally separate from production `services/`.

Production must stay stable; research must stay free. Ideas here (entropy theory,
epidemiology, game theory, ecology, astronomy, RL, GNNs, multi-agent sims) are
allowed to be messy and speculative. When an idea is proven — clean interface,
beats incumbent out-of-sample after costs, tested, observable — it graduates into
`libraries/` (reusable math) and `services/` (production wiring).

See [`docs/future_research.md`](../docs/future_research.md) for the research
agenda and promotion criteria.
